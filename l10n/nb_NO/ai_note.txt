Target language: Norwegian Bokmål. English name: Norwegian Bokmål.
Endonym: "norsk bokmål", one of the two official written standards of Norway.

THIS IS NOT NYNORSK. Nynorsk is the other written standard, locale code "nn".
They are close enough that a translation can drift into the other one; keep
this file consistently Bokmål.
