Entry msgstrs come from the Slovak locale (sk). Slovak ("slovenčina", spoken in
Slovakia) is a different language from Slovenian ("slovenščina", locale sl).
Flag a msgstr as repair with issue wrong_language and language "Slovenian" only
when it is demonstrably Slovenian, and cite the telling word in the reason.

Slovak uses the letters ľ, ĺ, ŕ, ô, ť, ď, ň; Slovenian never uses them.
Slovak declines with a genitive ("Nastavenia", "ponuka zvýraznenia"); Slovenian
uses a dative ("Nastavitve", "označba").

Words that are Slovenian, not Slovak (Slovak form after the slash):
- Uporabi / Použiť
- Prevajanje / Preklad (the verb is Preložiť)
- Prilagojeni pozivi / Vlastné prompty
- V redu / OK
- Posodobi / Aktualizovať
- Nastavitve / Nastavenia
- Odstrani / Odstrániť
- Ustvari / Vytvoriť
- Naprava / Zariadenie
- Počisti / Vyčistiť
- Prevajdi / Preložiť
- Kopiraj / Kopírovať
- Izberi / Vybrať
- označba / zvýraznenie

Many correct Slovak strings share a word or a form with Slovenian: "da", "ne",
"OK" and most technical terms are identical in both. Only a Slovenian-only word
from the list above, or one of the Slovak-only letters used in a Slovenian
context, justifies a wrong_language verdict. When the signal is not explicit,
abstain rather than repair.
